## Caption

When something hurts you,
your first instinct is often
to search for reasons.

Why did this happen?
Who caused it?
Why me?

But sometimes,
chasing answers only keeps you
closer to the pain.

The smarter question is not always
“why did this happen?”

It is—
what needs my attention now?

Your peace.
Your recovery.
Your next step.
Your lesson.
Your control.

Some answers may never come clearly.

But your healing
should not wait for them.
