## Caption

During a conversation on the Divya Jain Podcast (@divsjain), educator Avadh Ojha (@ray.avadhojha), spoke about why transformation requires tapasya. He compared personal growth to the process of shaping glass, where intense heat is required to change its form.

👉🏼 You Can’t Transform Your Life Without Going Through Tapasya

Ojha explained that just as glass must be heated to a high temperature before it can be reshaped, a person also has to go through difficult phases to bring meaningful change in their life.

He emphasised that wanting to change is not enough — discipline, struggle and tapasya are part of the process of becoming a different version of yourself.

👉🏼 The Logical Indian’s Perspective
