## Caption

Not every rule deserves
to be followed forever.

Sometimes, people keep repeating
old beliefs simply because
someone before them did the same.

Nobody questions the reason.
Nobody checks if it still makes sense.
Nobody asks whether it is helping
or only limiting everyone.

That is how society gets stuck.

One generation follows a rule
out of fear.
The next follows it out of habit.
And slowly, the reason disappears
but the restriction remains.

Progress begins
when someone finally asks—

Why are we still doing this?

Because blindly following tradition
is not respect.
Thinking for yourself is growth.
