## Caption

A son and a daughter
can grow up with the same mother
yet remember her very differently.

A son may see someone
who managed everything,
protected the family,
and never allowed the home to fall apart.

But a daughter may also notice
what that strength cost her.

The ambitions she postponed.
The needs she stopped expressing.
The parts of herself
that slowly disappeared behind responsibility.

That is why one may wish
for a partner like his mother,
while the other may fear
living the same life.

The lesson is not
to stop admiring mothers.

It is to create a life
where a woman can care for her family
without losing herself.
