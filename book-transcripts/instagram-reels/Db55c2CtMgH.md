## Caption

Some people become part of your memory
in the most ordinary ways.

A particular coffee.
A favorite chocolate.
A snack you always shared.

And suddenly, seeing that one thing
is enough to make someone appear in your mind.

That’s what makes small gestures
in relationships so meaningful.

When someone remembers exactly
what you like to eat or drink,
it may seem insignificant from the outside.

But underneath it is something deeper.

They noticed you.

They remembered the tiny details
you probably never asked them to remember.

Sometimes affection isn’t expressed
through grand surprises or expensive gifts.

Sometimes it’s simply someone showing up
with your favorite snack and saying,
without actually saying it,

“I thought of you.”
