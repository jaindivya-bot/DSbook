## Caption

Indian crab mentality
is definitely real.

Sometimes,
people are okay
with where they are…

until they see someone else
doing better.

You may be fine
with your Maruti.

But the day your friend
or neighbour brings a Skoda,
suddenly you feel
you need to do better too.

And instead of growing,
people start pulling
each other down.

That comes from
a scarcity mindset.

When we see someone winning,
we feel affected.

But the answer is simple—
wear blinders.

Don’t look ahead.
Don’t look back.

Do your own work.

And when you start moving forward,
keep it quiet.

You don’t need to share
your success everywhere.

Keep your success to yourself.
Share it only with
your close ones.
