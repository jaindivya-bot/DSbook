## Caption

As children,
we often see our parents
through a very small window.

We notice the rules.
The restrictions.
The mistakes.
The moments they disappointed us.

But as we grow older,
something changes.

We begin to understand
the pressure they carried,
the sacrifices they made,
and the decisions they took
with limited choices and limited knowledge.

They were not superheroes.
They were human beings
trying to figure life out
while raising another life.

Maturity begins
when judgment turns into understanding.

And sometimes,
the older you get,
the more grateful you become.
