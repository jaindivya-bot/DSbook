## Caption

You may not be permanent
in every part of someone else’s life.

Workplaces can replace you.
Friends can move ahead.
Families can shift priorities.
Relationships can change.

And that truth can feel harsh.

But there is one place
where nobody can replace you—
your own life.

Still, most people spend years
trying to become unforgettable
for everyone else,
while forgetting themselves.

Your life cannot be lived
by your office, your friends,
your family, or your partner.

Only you can live it.

So stop trying to become
irreplaceable everywhere.

Start becoming present
in your own life first.
