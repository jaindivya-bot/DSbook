## Caption

In India,
many boys are taught
to control girls
in the name of culture,
values, and manners.

“Why did you apply lipstick?”
“What is the need of gloss?”
“Focus on studies instead.”

But these things
are not connected.

A girl can apply gloss
and still be a topper.

She can dance
and still top in physics.

You don’t need to taunt her.
You don’t need to control her.
You don’t need to “protect” her
all the time.

If you actually talk to her,
you’ll understand—

she is just a normal human being.

Sometimes caring.
Sometimes moody.
Sometimes strong.
Sometimes vulnerable.

Not perfect.
Human.

And once you understand that,
your judgement automatically changes.
