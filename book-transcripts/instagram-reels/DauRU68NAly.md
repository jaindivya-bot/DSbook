## Caption

Rain has a strange way
of slowing everything down.

The noise becomes quieter.
The rush feels less important.
And for a moment,
you are left alone with your thoughts.

Maybe that is why rainy days
feel different.

They create space
for emotions we usually ignore.
The feelings we postpone.
The thoughts we keep buried
under busy schedules.

Sometimes, you do not need
to solve everything.

You just need to pause,
acknowledge what you are feeling,
and let it move through you.

Not every emotion is meant
to be carried forever.

Some are meant to be released.
