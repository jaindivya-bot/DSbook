## Caption

Being a nice person
is not the real problem.

The problem begins
when you try to stay kind, fair,
and thoughtful
in a crowd that has normalised cruelty.

When everyone around you
is acting without thinking,
your goodness can make them uncomfortable.

Not because you are wrong—
but because your presence exposes
what they have stopped questioning.

And sometimes,
people don’t attack you
because you are bad.

They attack you
because you remind them
they could have been better.

So choose your environment carefully.

The wrong people
can punish you
for having the right heart.
