Doing the right thing
does not always make you accepted.

When a group has followed
the same harmful belief for years,
anyone who questions it
can look like a threat.

Not because they are wrong—

what everyone else has normalised.

Sometimes, people would rather
silence a wiser voice
than examine their own behaviour.

So being good is not enough.
You must also understand
the environment you are standing in.

Choose carefully where you speak,
who you trust,
and which battles deserve your energy.

Because the wrong crowd
can punish you
for having the right intentions.
