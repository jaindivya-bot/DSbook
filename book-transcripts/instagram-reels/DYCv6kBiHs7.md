## Caption

“Are you okay, son?”
“Yes mom, I’m fine.”

Men are not afraid
of emotions.

They’re afraid
of what happens
after they show them.

Somewhere growing up,
they learned—

Silence is safer.

If you talk too much,
you look weak.

If you feel too much,
you look soft.

So they learn
to laugh it off.
Change the topic.
Keep everything inside.

They’ll talk about work.
Money.
Plans.
Future.

But not about
what’s actually hurting them.

And it’s not that
men don’t feel deeply.

They do.

They were just never taught
that feeling safe
is okay.

So they carry everything quietly…

Until one day
it comes out
as anger, distance,
or complete silence.
