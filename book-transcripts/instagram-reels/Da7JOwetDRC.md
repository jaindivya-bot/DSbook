## Caption

People often debate
who gains more in a relationship.

Who married better.
Who brought more value.
Who got the better deal.

But those questions miss
what actually makes a relationship last.

Life is constantly changing.

Income can rise or fall.
Status can disappear.
Looks evolve with time.

If a relationship is built only on those things,
it becomes fragile.

The strongest couples focus on something else.

They challenge each other to grow.
They support each other’s goals.
And they become better together
than they could have become alone.

The real win is not finding someone
above you or below you.

It’s finding someone
who wants to keep climbing with you.
