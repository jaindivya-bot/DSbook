## Caption

Life is like a train journey.
People are just passengers.
Some get off at the very first station.
Some travel with you for a while,
building beautiful memories together.
But when their stop arrives,
they leave.
The problem starts
when we try to force everyone
to stay until the very last stop.
But you can’t force anyone to stay.
Every person has their own destination.
So, when someone leaves,
don’t look at it as a loss.
Maybe their station simply arrived.
The right people will stay.
Enjoy the ones who are with you now.
Cherish the memories you’ve made.
Don’t hold on to what’s meant to go.
Sometimes,
letting go is the only way:
To keep moving forward.
