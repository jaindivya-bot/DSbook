## Caption

Kindness without boundaries
can easily be misunderstood.

At first, people may seem careful,
respectful,
and considerate.

But when they realise
there is no resistance,
no reaction,
and no clear limit,
some people start testing
how far they can go.

That is why being good
does not mean allowing
everything.

Your silence should not become
someone else’s permission.

Your patience should not become
an excuse for disrespect.

And your kindness should never
come at the cost
of your self-respect.

Set boundaries before people
start treating your tolerance
like an open invitation.
