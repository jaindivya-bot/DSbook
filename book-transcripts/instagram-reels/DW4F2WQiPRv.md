## Caption

We try to manage everything.
Even love.

We hold back.
We give… then stop.
Because somewhere we believe:
“If I love too much, they won’t value me.”

So we create distance.
We calculate.
We try to maintain importance.

Maybe it works for a moment.
But love doesn’t grow like that.

If you ration what you give,
what comes back will also be limited.

So stop doing the math.
Stop holding back.

Give attention.
Give love.

Leave the maths for the classroom.
Love with your heart, not your mind.
