## Caption

During a conversation on the Divya Jain Podcast, host Divya Jain (@divsjain ), spoke with educator Avadh Ojha (@ray.avadhojha ), about why struggle is an inevitable part of life and how embracing it can make a person mentally stronger.

👉🏼 Struggle Is a Part of Life
Avadh Ojha said that instead of constantly resisting difficult phases, people should learn to embrace them. According to him, struggles are not obstacles to life—they are a natural part of it and play an important role in shaping an individual's character.

👉🏼 Why He Mentioned Lord Krishna
Drawing from the life of Lord Krishna, Ojha explained that despite facing immense hardships from birth, Krishna never remained trapped in self-pity. He believes that learning to face challenges with resilience, rather than constantly complaining, helps build inner strength. He concluded by saying that the day a person learns to enjoy the process of struggle is the day they become "invincible."

👉🏼 The Logical Indian's Perspective
Challenges are an unavoidable part of life, but different people cope with them in different ways depending on their circumstances and support systems. While resilience can help people navigate adversity, seeking help when needed is equally important. Conversations like these encourage reflection on how we respond to difficult phases rather than viewing them only as setbacks.
