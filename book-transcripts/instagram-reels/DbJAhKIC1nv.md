Most people think happiness comes
from adding more to life.

More things.
More upgrades.
More possessions.

But every new item
demands a little of your attention.

It needs to be managed,
maintained, stored,
or replaced.

And over time,
what once felt like freedom
can start feeling like weight.

The problem isn’t owning things.

The problem is owning so much
that your belongings
begin owning your time and energy.

Sometimes, peace doesn’t come
from getting more.

It comes from removing
what no longer serves you.

Because the less clutter you carry,
the easier it becomes
to focus on what truly matters.
