## Caption

Most people want deep connection.

But they also want protection.

So they move closer,
then pull away.

They open up,
then build walls.

Not because they don’t care.
Because caring creates risk.

The closer someone gets,
the more power they have
to disappoint, hurt,
or change your life.

That’s why meaningful relationships
often feel complicated.

Real connection isn’t about finding someone
without flaws, insecurities, or baggage.

It’s about accepting that every person
comes with qualities that comfort you
and qualities that challenge you.

The strongest relationships aren’t perfect.

They’re built by two imperfect people
who decide that the connection
is worth the discomfort.
