the more your value starts dropping.

Smart people don’t try
to convince everyone.

their stance clear.

And the one
who doesn’t want to understand…
won’t understand even in 100 lines.

So stop justifying everywhere.

Sometimes,
silence is also an answer.

Respect comes from clarity,
not over-explanation.
