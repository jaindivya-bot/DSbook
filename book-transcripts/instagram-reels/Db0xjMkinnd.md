## Caption

Not everything you do creates an immediate result.

Sometimes you help someone
and never hear about it again.

You speak kindly to someone
and move on with your day.

You treat someone with respect
without expecting anything in return.

But the impact of those moments
doesn’t necessarily end there.

The way you treat people
becomes part of the experience
they carry forward.

And sometimes, much later,
life brings that same energy
back into your own world
through a completely different person
or situation.

That’s why kindness shouldn’t always
be measured by what you receive immediately.

Some things take time.

Keep putting good into the world
even when you can’t see the return yet.
