## Caption

Invite people.

To dinner.
To a friendly cricket match.
To a walk.

Connection doesn’t need
big plans or perfect timing.
It just needs intention.

And for people
your physical body can’t reach —
send small, thoughtful gestures.
A message.
A note.
A reminder that they matter.

You don’t need long conversations.
You don’t need explanations.

Sometimes,
a simple invitation
is enough to keep a bond alive.

And here’s the surprising part —
you’ll feel lighter doing it.

More connected.
More human.

Relationships grow
when you invite people in —
not when you wait for them to come.
