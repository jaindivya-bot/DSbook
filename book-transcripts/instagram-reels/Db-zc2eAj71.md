## Caption

Ever taken a photo of yourself
and immediately thought,
“Do I really look like that?”

The interesting part is,
other people may think that photo
looks completely normal.

The difference comes down to familiarity.

You spend years becoming comfortable
with one particular view of your own face.

So when a camera shows you
a slightly different version,
your brain notices every unfamiliar detail.

That doesn’t necessarily mean
the photograph looks worse.

It simply means
it isn’t the version of yourself
you’re most accustomed to seeing.

Meanwhile, your friends have spent years
seeing you from their perspective,
so that version feels more natural to them.

Sometimes what we call “better looking”
is simply what our brain knows best.
