👉🏼 "You Are Not Just One Personality"

Kshitij said people often define themselves with labels like "I'm shy" or "I'm an introvert." According to him, these labels are influenced by upbringing, habits, life experiences and social conditioning rather than being fixed traits. He suggested that personality is flexible and can change as people grow.

👉🏼 Why Labels Can Be Limiting

To explain his point, he said someone who considers themselves shy may still feel comfortable and expressive around close friends, while an outgoing person may naturally become quiet in certain situations. According to him, this shows that people adapt their behaviour based on context rather than fitting into a single personality type.
