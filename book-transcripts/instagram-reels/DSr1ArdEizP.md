## Caption

There are three areas in life
where you should listen to your parents —
but not blindly follow them.

First: Where you want to live.
Your city, your environment, your space
should align with your goals and aspirations.
Stay with them or live separately —
but make that decision consciously.

Second: Your faith.
Not just religion —
your belief system, your values,
what you truly stand for.
That clarity has to come from within.

Third: Your life partner.
Take their inputs.
Understand their concerns.
But the final decision must be yours.

Because the life you live,
the marriage you enter,
and the values you follow —
you will be responsible for them.

Advice can be shared.
Responsibility cannot.

Do you agree some choices must be personally owned? 👇
