[0:00] If you tend to get angry often, you have a desire for a certain thing. There is a thought flow going from you to that particular object. When that thought flow is interrupted by an obstacle, your thought flow gets deflected towards the obstacle and that is called anger. It means there are lots of desires within you that are getting blocked.
