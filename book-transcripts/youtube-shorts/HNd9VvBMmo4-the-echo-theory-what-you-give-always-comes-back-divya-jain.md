[0:00] Has anyone told you about the Echo Theory? Whenever you shout into the mountains, your voice always comes back. Maybe not immediately, but it comes back. Life also works in a strangely similar way. The kindness you show, the respect you give, the words that you speak, they don't just disappear. People don't always remember what you gave them, but they never forget how you made them feel. So the Echo Theory is pretty simple. The energy you send into the world doesn't disappear. It just takes its own time to find its way back to you.
