Idea 10 -  The 10-10-10 rule for decision making!

If you are stuck on to something, this 10-10-10 rule will change the game for you


Hi I am Divya, This is day 10 of sharing shit you should know but don’t, so hear it from a 40 year old!


The 10-10-10 rule is a decision-making framework popularized by author and journalist Suzy Welch. 


Ye involve karta hai considering the consequences of a decision in three timeframes: the immediate future (10 minutes), the near future (10 months), and the distant future (10 years).


For example - Apko ek Rs. 50,00,000 ka ghar lena hai
Ap aap socho - 10 min me iss decision se kya hoga?


10 months me kya hoga
And 10 years me kya hoga


10 mins me iss decision se apko khushi ho sakti hai
10 months me shayad apko cash crunch feel ho sakta hai if you have not saved up enough
10 years me shayad app ek 30 years ke loan ke sath kahi move nai kar paoge 
So before you move ahead - 10 min ki khushi ke aage socho
10 months and 10 years ka socho
If it makes sense - go ahead
If not - don’t waste even 10 mins on it
Hope this helps!
Caption: 

Have you ever been called indecisive? In this world jaha hamare paas itni choices hai, it is important to make the best decisions.

 Education system is inadequate in India 


Imagine karo ek aisa desh jahan education dreams ka raasta hai, par system ne ussi darwaze ko band kar rakha hai.

Rural students often face a harsh reality: poor infrastructure aur qualified teachers ki kam


Rote learning dominate karta hai, creativity aur critical thinking ko daba deta hai.
Exams become the end-all, be-all, jo pressure krta hai students ko to just score and not learn. 


Competition bohot zyada hai. Students ke stress levels skyrocket karte hain kyunki top institutions mein limited seats ke liye race chalti hai


Practical skills aur vocational training? Inko aksar ignore kiya jaata hai, jiski wajah se real-world readiness mein gap aa jata hai.


But guess what? Hamari government ne kuch Initiatives liye hai jisse ye problems khatam ho sakte hain : 


           1. Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan, 2018 mein launch hua, pre-school se class 12    tak holistic education ke liye, jo teacher training, technology aur infrastructure ko improve karke quality badhata hai. 


           2. NEP(National Education policy) 2020 Bharat ke education system mein major reform hai, jo rote learning se critical thinking aur creativity ki taraf shift karta hai, aur inclusive education, vocational training, aur teachers ke continuous professional development par zor deta hai.
          3.  Sarkar ne quality education aur better infrastructure ke liye kai digital initiatives launch kiye hain, jaise DIKSHA (national platform for school education), SWAYAM (online courses platform), aur PM eVIDYA (multi-mode education access). 


Umeed hai ki with continued efforts, curriculum reforms, better teacher training, aur increased funding ke saath, change possible hai.
